This task involves studying the launch of the "Channels" folder for the period 06.07.2026 — 24.08.2026 using training data.

The whole representation of the dashboard is available at the link: https://datalens.yandex/mz6iinw2440y5?_share_link=public

The findings include the following: 
* During the observed period, new subscribers across all acquisition channels most likely came from ad purchases or bot-driven inflation (fake followers), since Graph 1 shows sharp fluctuations between subscriptions and unsubscriptions, as well as a slight increase over the period. It is assumed that ads were launched during the peaks, so the quality and engagement of the audience are questionable.

* Active users have been subscribing less frequently: according to Graph 2, there are only 2–3 subscriptions per 100 active days, with a downward trend.

* After the release, Graph 3 shows a steady inflow of subscriptions from the folder — on average 25% per day.

* Subscriptions via the folder come mainly from iOS owners, which according to Graph 4 accounts for 17%.



The graph 1 «Dynamics of the total number of subscribers across all channels» is represented as a line graph based on subs_total data collected over the period.
